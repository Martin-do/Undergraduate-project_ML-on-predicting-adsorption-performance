# Paper 1 — Figure Captions V1

These captions correspond to the deterministic figures generated by `paper1/manuscript/build_figures.py`. Numerical figure values are read from the frozen multi-dataset registry; Figure 1 and Figure 5 are conceptual schematics.

## Figure 1. Evidence and provenance hierarchy used in the multi-dataset reanalysis

Flow of evidence from hierarchical literature-derived adsorption data through the frozen outcome-neutral protocol and into four evidence classes: three primary matched computational corpora, the lineage-overlapping Moosavi sensitivity, independent cross-team published corroboration from Aguiar and Kasemodel (2026), and the positive publication-separated comparator of Huang et al. (2026). Dataset labels show observations / contributing primary studies for the matched corpora. The figure emphasizes that provenance and source-lineage classification precede interpretation of validation results; Moosavi is therefore retained as a sensitivity analysis but not counted as an independent replication.

## Figure 2. Matched performance changes when the validation unit changes

Representative within-corpus R² estimates under shuffled row-random validation and primary-study GroupKFold validation. Each line holds the modelling population, predictor definition and model specification fixed while changing the unit withheld during validation. Dataset A uses XGBoost (273 observations / 24 studies), Liu dye uses CatBoost500 (624 / 17), Liu ammonia-N uses CatBoost500 (409 / 7), and Moosavi uses the published nine-variable random-forest specification (344 recoverable observations / 12 references). The dashed Moosavi line denotes lineage overlap with Dataset A and is excluded from the independent-replication count. The figure compares estimands rather than declaring either validation design universally correct.

## Figure 3. Validation-gap magnitude and primary-study count

Descriptive comparison of ΔR² = R²(row-random) − R²(primary-study grouped) with the number of contributing primary-study groups for the representative matched cases. The plot is intended to show corpus structure alongside validation sensitivity, not to imply a causal relationship between group count and ΔR². With only three primary matched corpora plus one lineage sensitivity, no cross-dataset trend model or correlation is fitted.

## Figure 4. Leave-one-study-out robustness

Pooled study-LOSO R² for the representative models in the matched corpora. LOSO removes one primary study at a time and therefore provides a direct robustness check for transfer to unseen studies. Pooled R² can be dominated by large or high-capacity studies and should be interpreted together with study-level errors and group-size distributions; it is not a substitute for per-study diagnostics.

## Figure 5. Claim–validation alignment for literature-derived adsorption machine learning

Conceptual distinction between two valid but different prediction targets. Row-random validation permits observations from the same study to appear in training and validation and therefore estimates interpolation to additional rows from represented systems. Primary-study grouped validation withholds all observations from a study and therefore estimates transfer to an unseen primary study. The appropriate validation unit depends on the scientific generalisation claim; group-aware evaluation is not presented as universally superior for every prediction task.
