## Novelty implication

The verified literature shows that ML-assisted adsorption/biochar optimization already exists. Therefore the reconstructed conference paper does **not** claim novelty merely from coupling ML and optimization. Its defensible contribution is the study-aware reliability re-evaluation of ID-SEAD, the matched random-versus-study-grouped evidence, the computational-lineage findings, and the prospective reliability gate for adsorption inverse design.
